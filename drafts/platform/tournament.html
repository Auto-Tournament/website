The spring LAN's main event: eight teams, two days, double elimination, so every team gets a second chance before it's out.

Matches run on the LAN servers and start as soon as both teams are connected. Captains do the map veto in the browser. The grand final is played on stage on Sunday evening and streamed.

Rules

  1. Be connected and ready 10 minutes after your match is called, or the admins may award a forfeit.
  2. Two tactical pauses per team per map, 60 seconds each. Technical pauses go through an admin.
  3. Coaches may talk during pauses and between halves only.
  4. Disputes go to the tournament admins in Discord before the next map starts.
